Bold minimalist flat vector-style near-black (#06120b) symbol centered inside a mint-green rounded square, subtle gradient #5fcf8c upper left to #3a9c63 lower right. Tile fills 85% of image, corner radius about 25% of tile width, outer background solid charcoal #0f1012. Intended for a tiny 30x30 pixel logo box in a dark UI: use very few bold forms, wide gaps and clean edges, substantial symbol filling about 65% of tile. Pure configuration theme. No letters, words, arrows, swords, shields, controllers, medieval motifs, decorative detail, glow, shadows, texture, beveling, or 3D. Deliver just the logo, no presentation board or labels. Primary request: Three thick vertical adjustment tracks of equal length, with chunky rounded rectangular knobs set high on the left, low in the middle, and midway on the right. Knobs merge cleanly with the tracks into three solid dark silhouettes. Beautiful optical rhythm, simple memorable shape. No outline boxes around knobs.
